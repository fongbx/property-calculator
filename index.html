What the words mean

BSD — Buyer's Stamp Duty
Tax on buying the home. It is worked out in steps: 1% on the first S$180,000, then 2%, 3%, 4%, 5% and 6% on each higher slice. A dearer home is not all taxed at the top rate.
ABSD — Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty
A second tax, as a flat percentage of the price. A common guide, which you should confirm because profiles differ: Singapore citizen first home 0%, second 20%, third or later 30%; permanent resident first home 5%, second 30%, third or later 35%; foreigner 60%; company or entity 65%. 35% is not in the spreadsheet's dropdown, so type it under Other. Official rates: IRAS stamp duty.
LTV — Loan-to-value
The share of the price the bank lends. The rest of the price, plus stamp duty, is paid with cash and CPF together.
IWAA — Income-weighted average age
The buyers' ages averaged by salary, so a higher earner counts more. If that age plus 30 years would pass 65, the spreadsheet cuts the loan limit from 75% to 55%. With no salary entered, it keeps a 30-year tenure and a 75% limit.
TDSR — Total Debt Servicing Ratio
All monthly debt, including this new repayment, should stay within 55% of gross monthly income. The minimum salary here is that repayment plus your other debt, divided by 0.55.
MSR — Mortgage Servicing Ratio
A stricter 30% test used for HDB flats and executive condominiums. It is shown only for comparison. It ignores other debt. Divide the repayment by 0.3.
Show funds
If income is short of the TDSR salary, a bank may ask to see savings. Unpledged money is only counted at 30%, so the sum is the gap times 48 months, divided by 0.3. Pledged money is locked for 48 months and counted in full: the gap times 48. Both are rounded up to the next S$1,000. With no salary entered, the whole minimum salary is the gap.
Option fee
On a new launch the spreadsheet uses 5% to receive the option. Of that, 1.25% is forfeited if you pull out, and 3.75% is refundable. On a resale, this app uses the usual 1% option fee, forfeited if you do not exercise, then 4% when you do.
TOP and CSC
Temporary Occupation Permit is when you can move into a new project. CSC is the legal completion certificate. In the stage table they are the last two calls for payment.
